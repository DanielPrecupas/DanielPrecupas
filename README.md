AI Systems Developer & Researcher working across machine learning, LLM applications, computer vision, and distributed systems.

I recently completed an MSc in Artificial Intelligence & Distributed Computing, with research focused on Transformer-based anomaly detection for decentralized finance. I enjoy taking ideas from papers and turning them into systems that actually run, whether that's an ML pipeline, a RAG application, a computer vision project, or an experimental research prototype.

## 🛠️ What I work on

- **Machine learning** — transformers, anomaly detection, deep learning, classical ML
- **LLMs and RAG** — retrieval pipelines, embeddings, wiring LLMs into real systems
- **Computer vision** — image and video processing, detection and recognition
- **Backend** — Python, APIs, Docker, cloud tooling
- **Research** — applied AI, optimization, intelligent systems
